#define SC16IS7XX_DRV_CMD_SET_BREAK   0x01

Driver-specific commands for the NXP SC16IS7xx I2C UART bridge.

Pass to uart_drv_cmd(). Requires CONFIG_UART_DRV_CMD=y.

Assert (p != 0) or deassert (p == 0) the LCR break-control bit.

While asserted, the bridge forces its TX line to logic 0 and holds it there. Required by peripherals that expect the host to park their RX input low while idle - notably the Piketronic RPP-U radon probe, which enters power-save when its RX_in is 0 and only re-arms on a 0 -> 1 transition held for 2 s.
